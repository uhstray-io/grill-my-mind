# Define what activating one branch authorizes

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: none
Blocked by: none
Parent: [Grill My Mind: find the architecture and build a resumable exploration skill](../map.md)

## Question

What research, questioning, duration, and usage limits belong to one user-activated branch? Decide when it pauses for answers, how cancellation/retry works, and how new suggested directions are shown. New branches must still wait for their own user activation; research completion must not imply acceptance of its conclusions.

## Answer

The user accepted one activation authorizing that branch's investigation until findings or a question require attention. New suggested directions remain inactive. Refine the experience based on use rather than extending the planning phase. Numerical time/cost budgets remain a trial consideration; no unlimited autonomous expansion is authorized.
