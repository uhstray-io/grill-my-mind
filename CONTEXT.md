# Grill My Mind

Grill My Mind supports systematic exploration of an idea through a persistent visual mind map, questions, and agent research. A map can be reopened and continued across sessions and understood by other agents.

## Language

**Mind map**:
The persistent body of an idea's exploration, including related thoughts, questions, research, and relationships. It remains useful while exploration is ongoing; it does not require a final handoff.

**Suggested branch**:
A direction for further exploration identified by an agent and presented to the user. Suggestion alone does not authorize exploring that branch.

**Branch activation**:
The user's explicit selection of a suggested branch through the interface to begin exploring it.

**Exploration session**:
A period of working with a mind map. Ending a session does not finish the map or prevent a later session from continuing it.
